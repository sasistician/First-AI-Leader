The Program 02 — 03
the five-week climb · what each week explores and builds

Five weeks to go from AI user to AI leader — and drive AI transformation across your organisation.

Week 0 gets Claude Code running before we begin — no coding required: you direct, the AI does the work. Nothing resets; each week stacks on the last, and most leaders start reclaiming hours within the first fortnight.

01
Week 01 · Mindset your first agent
Explore

From prompting a chatbot to directing an agent — the agentic loop, what a "tool" is, and why context is the whole game. The reframe: you now manage AI labour.

You build

Automate one real recurring task from your own week — inbox triage, a meeting-prep brief, a research digest.→ Leave with: one recurring task off your plate — zero code written.

02
Week 02 · Knowledge grounding
Explore

Why generic models give generic answers. How agents get grounded — project files & memory, skills, and retrieval (RAG). What MCP is: how the AI plugs into your tools and data.

You build

Connect Claude to your own documents and give it a persistent memory of how you work.→ Leave with: an AI that knows your business and answers reliably about it.

03
Week 03 · Orchestration delegation
Explore

Orchestration is management. Designing sub-agents, when to delegate vs. do, chaining multi-step workflows, giving agents reach via MCP tools, failure modes & the human-in-the-loop.

You build

A multi-step workflow or a team of sub-agents that owns a recurring job end-to-end.→ Leave with: a delegated AI workflow your team can run end-to-end.

04
Week 04 · Strategy operating model
Explore

From personal use to org leverage — opportunity portfolios, value × feasibility prioritisation, build-vs-buy, ROI, risk & governance, and how to challenge vendors and technical teams.

You build

Your AI-First Opportunity Map and a prioritised 90-day roadmap for your organisation.→ Leave with: a defensible org-level AI roadmap.

05
Week 05 · Capstone ship & certify
Explore

What "production-ready" and "trustworthy" mean at exec altitude — evaluation, reliability, guardrails, security, and knowing when not to automate. Telling a real agent from a demo.

You build

Finalise and defend your capstone agent in a live online showcase.→ Leave with: a live AI product you own + the AI Leader certification.
