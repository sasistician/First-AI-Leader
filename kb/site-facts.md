## Why take this course

Reinvent yourself for the era of AI. Five weeks to stop chatting with AI and start directing it, across your own work and the teams you lead. Delegate whole jobs to a team of agents instead of prompting a chatbot one task at a time. Orchestration is just management.

Before and after: you chat with AI, then you direct AI. You prompt a chatbot, then you delegate to a team of agents. You do the work, then you orchestrate; that's just management.

## The five weeks of The AI-First Leader

Five weeks to go from AI user to AI leader, and drive AI transformation across your organisation.

- Week 01 · Mindset · Your first agent. From prompting a chatbot to directing an agent. Automate one real recurring task and leave with it off your plate, zero code written.
- Week 02 · Knowledge · Grounding. Connect Claude to your own documents and give it persistent memory of how you work. Leave with an AI that knows your business.
- Week 03 · Orchestration · Delegation. Design a multi-step workflow or team of sub-agents that owns a recurring job end-to-end: a delegated AI workflow your team can run.
- Week 04 · Strategy · Operating model. Build your AI-First Opportunity Map and a prioritised 90-day roadmap: a defensible org-level AI plan.
- Week 05 · Capstone · Ship & certify. Finalise and defend your capstone agent in a live showcase. Leave with a live AI product you own, plus certification.

## The AI-First Engineer

The session decks on this site (Week 0 to Week 5) are from the sibling program, The AI-First Engineer, a Claude Code builder program for software engineers covering foundations, Claude Code fundamentals, context engineering, Skills, MCP, and orchestration with guardrails and evaluation. Session decks are available in English and 中文.

Coding: The AI-First Leader needs no coding experience. The AI-First Engineer is for software engineers: its labs work in Python code repositories from the terminal (git, uv, pytest), although participants direct Claude Code to write much of the code rather than writing it all by hand.

## Glossary

- MCP stands for Model Context Protocol: an open standard for connecting Claude to external tools and data. It is covered in Week 4 of The AI-First Engineer.
- Claude Code is Anthropic's agentic coding tool; both programs are built on it.
- A Skill packages instructions Claude can load when relevant (Week 3 of The AI-First Engineer).
